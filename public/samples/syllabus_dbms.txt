DBMS Laboratory Syllabus (sample)
# Format per line:  Topic | SQL concepts | difficulty (easy/medium/hard) | learning objective
Selection and Filtering | SELECT, WHERE, AND | easy | Retrieve rows that satisfy several conditions
Sorting and Limiting | ORDER BY, LIMIT | easy | Order results and fetch top-N rows
Range and Pattern Search | BETWEEN, IN, LIKE | easy | Use range, list and pattern predicates
Aggregate Functions | COUNT, SUM, AVG, MAX | easy | Summarise a set of rows
Grouping | GROUP BY, HAVING | medium | Summarise per group and filter groups
Joins | INNER JOIN | medium | Combine rows of related tables
Outer Joins | LEFT JOIN | medium | Keep unmatched rows and detect missing relationships
Subqueries | SUBQUERY | medium | Use nested queries as values
Correlated Subqueries | CORRELATED SUBQUERY, EXISTS | hard | Write queries that depend on the outer row
Set Operations | UNION, INTERSECT, EXCEPT | medium | Combine result sets
Window Functions | RANK, OVER, PARTITION BY | hard | Rank and accumulate within partitions
Conditional Expressions | CASE | medium | Derive classes with CASE
Data Manipulation | INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE | medium | Change table data safely
Views | VIEW | medium | Create reusable virtual tables
Schema Definition | CREATE TABLE, PRIMARY KEY, NOT NULL | medium | Define tables with constraints
